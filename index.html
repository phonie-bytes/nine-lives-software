The studio

Nine Lives Software makes games and software. The games so far: a detective story and a farm where nature fights back. Every picture, note and word in them is generated in code.

We grew up on the old adventure games - the ones with a parser or a handful of verbs, puzzles you thought about on the bus, honest deaths and a save before every door. NOIR is our love letter to them: deductions you make yourself, and a few second chances if you've earned them.

We also build custom software: tools and applications made to order.

Idea, design and development by Pieter De Villiers, with the assistance of Claude and other tools.
