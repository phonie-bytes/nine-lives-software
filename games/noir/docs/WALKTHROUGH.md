## Other endings

- **The Hard Way** - arrest Brody with the evidence, but with honor below 25.
- **Justice, of a Kind** - shoot Brody with honor 0 or more.
- **The New Crane** - shoot Brody with honor below 0.
- If your honor drops below -30 before you bring Muldoon the evidence, he refuses to help, and a gun is the only way out of the study.
- **The Wrong Man** - believe the wrong clues and phone in the wrong killer (below).

Where the red herrings lead:

- *Big Tony killed Eddie* - phone it in to Brody from your office and Big Tony goes down for it: **The Wrong Man** ending.
- *The ledger is at the Pier 4 cannery* - the cab will take you. It's an ambush: shoot first with a loaded revolver (and lose some health), buy them off with Brody's envelope (and lose the evidence), or die. Talking only costs you seconds.
- *Muldoon is the bluecoat* - accuse him and the only honest cop in the precinct shuts the door on you; you'll face Brody alone, gun in hand.
- *Rita set Eddie up* (Vera the cigarette girl's red coat) - phone it in and Rita goes down for it: **The Wrong Woman** ending.
- *It was an accident* (Swede the sailor's story) - tell Brody you're dropping it: **The Easy Answer** ending.
- *Pike faked the log page* (Officer Dunn's whisper - Dunn is Brody's man) - tell Muldoon and he throws you out.
- *The ledger is in box 9 at the Opera House* (Mr. Fisk, Eddie's neighbor) - a wasted cab ride that costs forty minutes of the clock.

## Difficulty, time and health

| | Easy | Normal | Hard |
|---|---|---|---|
| Second chances | 5 | 3 | 1 |
| Seconds to act in a standoff | 20 | 10 | 6 |
| Act one deadline (Crane's letter to the Pier 9 standoff) | none | 240 s | 120 s |
| Act two deadline (Rita's call to Crane's study) | none | 900 s | 480 s |
| Current lead in the notebook | yes, plus a gut feeling about which cards to connect | yes | no |
| TAB labels | yes | yes | no |

The deadline clocks only run while you're playing - not while text is on screen. Once midnight passes, Kane's health drains until he reaches the end of the act (or dies). Loading the revolver in a standoff costs 4 seconds; reading an item costs 1.5. A nap on the office couch gives back health but costs a minute of the clock.

## Second chances

Kane has 3 (on Normal) second chances per game. After some deaths the death screen offers SECOND CHANCE, which puts you back just before the fatal move:

| Death | Saved if... |
|---|---|
| Shot | you carry Eddie's diary, Eddie's file or the ledger (it stops the bullet) |
| Drowned | you carry the flashlight (a fisherman sees it) |
| Beaten (or worn down after a deadline) | your honor is 0 or more (someone you did right by drags you out) |
| Run down | always - the Packard only clips you |
| Poisoned | you've read Eddie's note about the bartender |
| Drank yourself to death | never |
| Anything in Crane's study | never - the finale is one shot |
