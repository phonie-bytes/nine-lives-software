## Deductions

In the notebook, click one card and then another to connect them. Some deductions are required to go on; some are worth points; some are **wrong** - built on lies and planted evidence - and the notebook can't tell the difference (on Easy, doubtful cards get a "?").

| Connect | With | Deduces | True? |
|---|---|---|---|
| Eddie's note: the password is 'the hour I always... | Eddie phoned me every night at midnight sharp. '... | the password is the hour Eddie always called - M... | yes |
| Eddie 'drowned' - but he couldn't swim. Crane gi... | Eddie's file: 'Blue Moon. Crane. Follow the mone... | Eddie didn't drown by accident. He was following... | yes |
| Big Tony's knuckles are scabbed over - fresh, a... | Sal: Big Tony walked Eddie out the back on Tuesd... | Big Tony walked Eddie out back and beat him to d... | **no - a red herring** |
| Vera, the cigarette girl: Eddie left Tuesday wit... | Rita: Crane had Eddie killed. She gave me the ke... | Rita lured Eddie out to his death - and now she'... | **no - a red herring** |
| Swede, a sailor: saw Eddie walk off Pier 9 drunk... | Eddie 'drowned' - but he couldn't swim. Crane gi... | Eddie got drunk and fell off the pier. An accide... | **no - a red herring** |

Where the red herrings lead:

- *Big Tony killed Eddie* - phone it in to Brody from your office and Big Tony goes down for it: **The Wrong Man** ending.
- *Rita set Eddie up* (Vera the cigarette girl's red coat) - phone it in and Rita goes down for it: **The Wrong Woman** ending.
- *It was an accident* (Swede the sailor's story) - tell Brody you're dropping it: **The Easy Answer** ending.

## Difficulty, time and health

| | Easy | Normal | Hard |
|---|---|---|---|
| Second chances | 5 | 3 | 1 |
| Seconds to act in a standoff | 20 | 10 | 6 |
| Deadline (Crane's letter to the Pier 9 standoff) | none | 240 s | 120 s |
| Current lead in the notebook | yes, plus a gut feeling about which cards to connect | yes | no |
| TAB labels | yes | yes | no |

The deadline clock only runs while you're playing - not while text is on screen. Once midnight passes, Kane's health drains until he reaches the end of the act (or dies). Loading the revolver in a standoff costs 4 seconds; reading an item costs 1.5. A nap on the office couch gives back health but costs a minute of the clock.

## Second chances

Kane has 3 (on Normal) second chances per game. After some deaths the death screen offers SECOND CHANCE, which puts you back just before the fatal move:

| Death | Saved if... |
|---|---|
| Shot | you carry Eddie's diary, Eddie's file or the ledger (it stops the bullet) |
| Drowned | you carry the flashlight (a fisherman sees it) |
| Beaten (or worn down after a deadline) | your honor is 0 or more (someone you did right by drags you out) |
| Run down | always - the Packard only clips you |
| Poisoned | you've read Eddie's note about the bartender |
| Drank yourself to death | never |
